How voting works

🪪
1. Verify

Enter your name, roll number, department and year.

🔵
2. Press

Press the blue button next to your candidate on the ballot unit.

🧾
3. Check slip

The VVPAT slip shows your choice for 5 seconds.

✅
4. Done

Your vote is saved. One roll number can vote only once.

Meet the candidates

A NOTA (None of the above) option is also on the ballot.

Rules

  • Every student can vote only once with their roll number.
  • Vote by yourself. Do not let anyone see or press the button for you.
  • Who you voted for is never saved with your name (secret ballot).
  • The final result is shown after the election officer closes voting.
